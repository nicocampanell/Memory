# c3-morph: one shape, never cut (16 beats)

Technique: one element the whole time. Only its size, radius, fill and contents change. If one thing disappears and another appears, it's a cut, not a morph.

```text

It starts on an accent dot in the centre of the canvas.
Beats 0-2: the dot grows into the real primary action button of feature 2 (kit/BRAND.md). A cursor glides in and clicks it on beat 2.
Beats 2-5: the button stretches into the real card or form that action opens. Its fields or rows appear inside it, one per beat.

Beats 8-13: the pill stretches into the next real screen in the flow. Three of its steps or items pop in on beats 9, 11 and 13, each with a ding.
Beats 14-16: the last item fills with the accent colour and grows into the real success state, with the success sound.

The shape is one element the whole time. Re-shoot every element on a transparent background so no page background shows on the moving shape. A colour change is a circle growing from the cursor, never a fade. Show me the beat map before you write any code.
```
