# c2-demo: the camera follows the cursor (20 beats)

Technique: one camera, zooming in log space. 1x→2x must feel as fast as 2x→4x.

```text
Make scenes/c2-demo/clip.mp4: a 20-beat screen demo of Abode Money's main feature (feature 1 in kit/BRAND.md), the product in kit/. Follow CLAUDE.md and LOOK.md, and use the tempo in kit/AUDIO.md.

Film it like a screen recording with a smart camera. The whole real screen sits on the canvas as one big card, and the camera zooms to wherever the cursor works. The cursor scales with the zoom.
Beats 0-3: wide on the real home screen. The cursor moves to the feature's entry point and clicks on beat 3.
Beats 3-5: the feature's page pushes in from the right. The camera zooms onto the card where the work happens, in its real "before" state.
Beat 6: the cursor performs the one action that turns the feature on, with a click.
Beats 7-13: the result fills in from the real page, one step every two beats, each with a soft ding.
Beats 14-17: the camera pulls back to the whole page, and a caption (the feature's benefit in five words or fewer) rises in at the top left, with one serif accent word.
Beats 18-20: hold, then the cursor leaves the frame.

If no screenshot has the "before" state, make it by editing a copy of the real page in kit/site, log the edit in kit/site/CHANGES.md, and shoot it. Show me three stills (beats 3, 6 and 16) before you render the full clip.
```
